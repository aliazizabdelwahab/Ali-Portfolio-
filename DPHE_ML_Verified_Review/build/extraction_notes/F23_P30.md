Title: Leakage and the reproducibility crisis in machine-learning-based science
Authors: Sayash Kapoor; Arvind Narayanan
Year 2023; Patterns 4(9), 100804; DOI 10.1016/j.patter.2023.100804 (OA)
Type: methodological/meta-science (not heat transfer; no thermal ML model).
Content: survey of 17 fields, 294 papers affected by leakage; taxonomy of 8 leakage types: L1.1 no test set; L1.2 pre-processing on train+test; L1.3 feature selection on train+test; L1.4 duplicates; L2 illegitimate features; L3.1 temporal leakage; L3.2 non-independence between train and test samples; L3.3 sampling bias in test distribution. Proposes "model info sheets". Civil-war case study: complex ML no better than logistic regression once leakage fixed.
Relevance: provides the checklist used in this review's leakage audit (L1.2, L1.4, L3.2 most relevant to factorial DPHE experiments and repeated readings).
Tier 3 (methodological support; retained because it is the reference standard for the validation critique, although it does not apply ML to thermal performance).
