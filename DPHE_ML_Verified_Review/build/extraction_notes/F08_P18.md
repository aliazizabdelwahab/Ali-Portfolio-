Title: Heat transfer and pressure drop of Al2O3/water nanofluid in conically coiled tubes: Experimental and artificial neural network prediction
Authors: Mathkar A. Alharthi; Bandar Awadh Almohammadi; M.A. Sharafeldin; Mohamed T. Abdelghany; Samir Bendoukha; H.A. Refaey
Year 2024; Case Studies in Thermal Engineering 54, 104043; DOI 10.1016/j.csite.2024.104043 (open access CC BY-NC-ND)
HX: conically coiled tube (CCT), single-stream test section, electrically heated (uniform heat flux) - NOT a DPHE
Data: experimental. Al2O3/water (mono), 50 nm, 0.3/0.6/0.9 vol%; 3 coils torsion 0.02/0.036/0.052; m 0.03-0.09 kg/s; Re 4836-12562; Tin 25 C.
Conditions: 3 conc x 3 coils x (number of flow rates not stated). Number of ML samples: Not reported.
ML: FFNN (MATLAB, TRAINLM, back-prop, MSE loss). Table 2: "Hidden layers 3", transfer function PURELIN (linear) -> network is effectively linear; neuron counts Not reported.
Inputs: mass flow rate, volume concentration, coil torsion (dimensional/mixed). Outputs: Nu (separate net for f).
Split: 70/15/15 random (train/validation/test), early stopping on validation. No CV (k-fold recommended as future work).
Metrics Nu: R=0.9952; Table 5: MSE 9.6166, RMSE 3.101, MAPE 2.6122% (abstract mislabels RMSE=9.6166). f: R=0.9482/0.9492 (inconsistent), MAPE 7.46%. Unclear whether test-only or all data.
Extrapolation claim: predicts phi=0.8% and 0.5% (no experiments) and says they "align" - no validation. These are unverified interpolations in concentration.
Uncertainty: experimental only (Re 1.4%, Nu 4.3%, f 3.5%). No predictive uncertainty.
Strength: clear property models; states inputs. Weakness: linear activation inconsistency, no sample count, random split, unvalidated predictions, metric labelling errors.
Leakage: random split of rows from same coil/conc grid; possible repeated readings - not reported.
Relevance: nanofluid + experimental + ANN but different geometry (coiled tube). Same group as P01 (Abdelghany/Sharafeldin).
Tier proposal: 2 (geometry differs) -> maybe 3. Data availability: on request.
