Title: Optimized design of Helical-Finned Double Pipe heat exchangers via numerical simulation and Artificial Intelligence
Authors: S. Morteza Mousavi; Aliakbar Sheikhi Azizi; Milad Razbin; A. Ali Rabienataj Darzi; Ming Li
Year 2025 (online Oct 2024); Applied Thermal Engineering 258, 124605; DOI 10.1016/j.applthermaleng.2024.124605
HX: DPHE with helical fins in annulus; water; CFD (ANSYS Fluent, k-omega SST, validated vs Dittus-Boelter <=4%, Petukhov <=8%).
Data: CFD, RSM-designed: 44 combinations (15 + 29 additional points). Inputs (4): Re (outer), number of fins, fin height, twist pitch. Outputs: Nu (thermal) and f (hydraulic) -> relative Nu_r, f_r, PEC.
Preprocessing: min-max normalisation to [a,b] before randomised split.
Validation: 39 train / 5 test, 9-fold CV used with "total goodness function" TGF (combining R2 and MSE of train and test) to select neurons (6-12).
Model: FFBP ANN 4-7-1 (separate nets Nu and f), tansig/purelin, LM, lr 0.6, momentum 0.9; MATLAB; weights & biases published (Tables 5-6) -> reproducible.
Metrics: R2 ~1 (exact test values in figures); sensitivity analysis of inputs (Re_out 35% for Nu).
Optimisation: single-objective GA (pop 50, 100 gens) -> optimum Nu_r 2.01, f_r 1.01, PEC 1.43; CFD verification of optimum reported.
Uncertainty: none.
Critical: CFD data (noise-free, deterministic) -> R2~1 expected; TGF uses test data in model selection (test not independent); 5-point test set; hydraulic targets central (outside our scope).
Strength: published weights (deployable/reproducible); k-fold CV; CFD confirmation of the GA optimum (closing the loop).
Tier 3 (CFD, geometry optimisation; useful for reproducibility/publishing weights & confirmation-run idea).
