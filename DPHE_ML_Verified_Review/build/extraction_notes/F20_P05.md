Title: Effect of using wire coils and aluminum oxide nanofluid on heat transfer in a double-pipe heat exchanger and predicting data with artificial neural networks
Authors: Roohallah Karimpooremam; Fatemeh Poursaied; Bahram Keyvani; Milad Razmi; Reza Aghayari; Davood Toghraie; Soheil Salahshour
Year 2025; Case Studies in Thermal Engineering 71, 106232; DOI 10.1016/j.csite.2025.106232 (CC BY OA)
HX: mini concentric double-pipe, horizontal, L 1200 mm; inner tube 6/8 mm, outer 14/16 mm; stainless; wire coils (p/d 0 [no coil],1,1.6,2.4; wire d 5 mm) in inner tube.
Fluids: hot = Al2O3/water NF (55 nm, SDS surfactant) 0.02/0.04/0.06 vol% in inner tube (reservoir w/ thermostat); cold = mains water in annulus (once-through).
Ranges: Re 4000-14000 (hot NF side). Hot inlet temperatures: "various" - values Not reported. Cold flow: Not reported.
Samples: 72 experimental Nu points (consistent with 3 conc x 4 p/d x 6 Re - inference). Repeats: Not reported.
Inputs: Re, phi, p/d (3). Output: Nu only (f measured but not modelled by ANN).
Model: MLP 3-22-1, LM, tansig hidden / purelin output, MATLAB (implied). ~111 weights for 72 points (ratio ~1.5 weights per sample).
Training: 11 epochs; training MSE fell to 3.77e-09 (memorisation). Split: Not reported. Validation/test: Not reported. Scaling: Not reported.
Baselines: two 2nd-order polynomial (Poly2D) correlations: Nu(Re,p/d) R2 0.9385, MSE 18.30; Nu(Re,phi) R2 0.85387, MSE 43.48.
ANN metrics (unclear if all data or test): "correlation coefficient" 0.983; MSE 11.2783; RMSE 3.3583 (Nu units, Nu range 60-130 => ~3-5%); MAE 0.7622; margin of deviation +3.42% / -3.21%.
Uncertainty: experimental Nu +-3.8%, Re +-3.5%, f +-4.1%. No predictive uncertainty. ANN RMSE ~ same order as Nu measurement uncertainty.
Validation of rig: Dittus-Boelter (water).
Critical: no split reported -> metrics probably on full dataset incl. training; 22 neurons on 72 points; training MSE ~0; ANN barely better than a 6-parameter quadratic in RMSE (3.36 vs 4.28) - modest gain for much higher capacity. "Data availability: No data was used" (boilerplate error).
Strength: reports physical-unit RMSE and polynomial baseline; DPHE + nanofluid + experimental; Re/phi inputs.
Relevance: high setup similarity (DPHE, Al2O3 NF, Nu target) but ML weak.
Tier 1. Not benchmark (no split).
