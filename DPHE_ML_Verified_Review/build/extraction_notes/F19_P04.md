Title: Employing ensemble machine learning techniques for predicting the thermohydraulic performance of double pipe heat exchanger with and without turbulators
Authors: S. Sammil; M. Sridharan
Year 2024; Thermal Science and Engineering Progress 47, 102337; DOI 10.1016/j.tsep.2023.102337
HX: counter-flow DPHE, copper, core length 0.5 m (Table 6 says L 1000 mm), inner 12.7 mm, outer 25.4 mm. Case 1 plain, Case 2 gear-disc turbulators (own experiments), Case 3 dolphin-ring turbulators (data taken from literature [52]).
Fluid: water both sides (no nanofluid). Hot inlet 77 C (text also says 65 C), cold inlet 12 C; hot Re 3000-13000 (Table 4 says 7000-12000), cold Re 5000. Inconsistent reporting.
Data: 540 samples (432 train / 108 test, random 80/20); 70% own experiments, 30% literature. Number of distinct operating conditions: Not reported -> 540 rows from 3 cases x Re range implies many repeated/time-series readings (leakage risk HIGH).
Inputs: Re, N (number of turbulators 0-8), L/D (length of turbulators 0-8) - 3 inputs. Outputs: Nu, f, effectiveness, NTU.
Models: RF, XGBoost, CatBoost (Python). Hyperparameters: grid/random/Bayesian search with k-fold CV (k not stated) - RF n_estimators 700, "learning_rate 0.3" (RF has no learning rate - reporting error), min_samples_leaf 2.5 (non-integer - error); CatBoost 150 trees lr 0.25 subsample 0.5 depth 11; XGBoost 200, lr 0.3, subsample 0.7, depth 14.
Feature importance: boosting importance (Re 0.89 for Nu).
Metrics (Table 8; units unclear -> RMSE ~0.01-0.05 must be on scaled targets or pooled): RF test AAPRE 0.59%, RMSE 0.0494, R2 0.9741; CatBoost test AAPRE 0.33%, RMSE 0.0217, R2 0.9989; XGBoost test AAPRE 0.49%, RMSE 0.0271, R2 0.9881. Abstract: CatBoost R2 0.9987, AAPRE 0.1837%. Overall "accuracy" 97-99%. Whether metrics pooled over 4 outputs: Not reported.
Abstract claims sensitivity analysis on "best model (Random Forest)" and leverage outlier analysis - not shown in body (contradiction).
Uncertainty: experimental Nu +-1.9%, f +-5.9%, Re +-3.9%. AAPRE (0.33%) << measurement uncertainty (1.9%) -> implausible as genuine generalization; consistent with near-duplicate rows in train/test.
Data availability: confidential. Leakage: HIGH (random split of rows from few conditions; mixed literature data).
Strength: multi-algorithm boosting comparison; hyperparameter search described; feature importance.
Weakness: no nanofluid; inconsistent specs; metric units unclear; implausible accuracy; literature data mixed in; no independent validation.
Tier 1 (DPHE + experimental + thermal ML) but low quality. Not benchmark.
