Title: Experimental investigation and ANN modeling of thermo-hydraulic characteristics of GO nanofluid in a tube equipped with wire-coil inserts
Authors: Ravindra Gupta; Priyanka Shah; Arvind Mohite; Nagendra Prasad Pandey; Rajkumari Soni; Niraj Tevar; Ankita Sharma; Hardik Solanki
Year 2026; Discover Nano 21, 352; DOI 10.1186/s11671-026-04805-1 (OA CC BY-NC-ND)
HX: circular tube with wire-coil inserts (P/D 1, 1.5, 2) - described as heat exchanger test section; NOT DPHE.
Fluid: GO/water 0.025-0.1 wt% (k and mu measured). Re 5,000-18,000.
Samples: 120 experimental datasets; random 70/15/15 -> 84/18/18.
Inputs: Re, GO wt%, P/D (3). Outputs: Nu and f jointly (one 2-output network).
Model: FFBP ANN 3-12-8-2, LM, tansig hidden / purelin output; architecture chosen on validation error; MATLAB implied; min-max normalisation (on all data before split - not stated otherwise). Converged at epoch 17 (Nu).
Metrics (Table 3, normalised scale): Nu R2 train 0.9929 / val 0.9869 / test 0.9912 / overall 0.9913; RMSE test 0.0255 (normalised), MAE test 0.0211. f test R2 0.8728.
Uncertainty: experimental (Table 2); no predictive uncertainty.
Critical: metrics only on normalised scale (no physical-unit error); random split of 120 rows over Re x conc x P/D grid; ~150 weights for 84 training rows; joint Nu+f output couples thermal and hydraulic fitting.
Strength: clear split counts, architecture, normalisation, validation-based selection.
