Title: Experimental and numerical investigation of heat transfer and flow of water-based graphene oxide nanofluid in a double pipe heat exchanger using different artificial neural network models
Authors: Fatemeh Zakeri; Mohammad Reza Sarmasti Emami
Year 2023; International Communications in Heat and Mass Transfer 148, 107002; DOI 10.1016/j.icheatmasstransfer.2023.107002
HX: counter-current concentric DPHE, copper inner tube 8/10 mm, outer 40/50 mm, L 0.5 m. Hot DI water in inner tube (66.66 mL/s fixed); cold GO/water NF in annulus.
Fluid: GO/water mono nanofluid, 0.01/0.055/0.1 (text alternates vol% and wt%), two-step + ultrasonic; stability visual 30 days.
Ranges: hot inlet 35/45/55 C; cold (NF) flow 14.4/18.9/23.4 mL/s; cold inlet T controlled (value not reported). Re: example 14,693 (text). 
Target HTC: computed via data reduction that applies Sieder-Tate-type correlations (Nu=0.027Re^0.8Pr^1/3(mu/mu_w)^0.14 for annulus/inner) with mixture-rule NF properties (Maxwell k, Einstein mu) - i.e. h is at least partly correlation-derived rather than independently measured ("experimental and numerical"). Critical: ML partially learns a closed-form correlation.
Design: RSM (Design-Expert; CCD listed in nomenclature). Number of runs: Not explicitly reported; ANOVA (model SS 15218.93, R2 0.95, F 98.43) implies ~20 runs (reviewer inference).
Inputs (3, dimensional): hot inlet T, concentration, cold flow rate. Output: HTC (h) only.
Models: MLPNN and RBFNN (MATLAB); architecture, neurons, activation, spread: "trial and error" - Not reported. Also RSM quadratic model (R2 0.95, adj 0.94, pred 0.91).
Preprocessing: min-max normalization (Eq. 30) - on full data before split (not stated otherwise).
Split: random 80/20 train/test; no validation set; neurons chosen by trial and error (likely using test).
Metrics (Table 7, W/m2K): MLPNN train MAE 5.472 RMSE 6.578 R2 0.96; test MAE 5.741 RMSE 8.153 R2 0.93; all RMSE 6.922 R2 0.95. RBFNN train MAE 1.499 RMSE 2.169 R2 0.996; test MAE 4.443 RMSE 5.370 R2 0.969; all RMSE 3.087 R2 0.991. HTC mean ~163 W/m2K -> test RMSE ~3.3% (RBF) / 5% (MLP).
Note: MLP test R2 0.93 is similar to RSM quadratic (R2 0.95, pred R2 0.91) - ANN adds little over quadratic regression.
Uncertainty: no experimental uncertainty analysis reported; no predictive uncertainty.
Strengths: DPHE + nanofluid + experimental operating inputs (Th,in, flow, phi) similar to ours; train/test metrics in physical units; RBF vs MLP comparison; RSM baseline.
Weaknesses: tiny dataset (~20), ~4 test points; correlation-derived target; random split of factorial/CCD grid; hyperparameters not reported; hot-side flow fixed.
Tier 1. Benchmark candidate (secondary) for input structure; not for target definition.
