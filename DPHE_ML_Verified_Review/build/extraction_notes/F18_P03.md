Title: Performance optimization of a heat exchanger with coiled-wire turbulator insert by using various machine learning methods
Authors: Nevin Celik; Beyda Tasar; Sinan Kapan; Vedat Tanyildizi
Year 2023; International Journal of Thermal Sciences 192, 108439; DOI 10.1016/j.ijthermalsci.2023.108439
HX: concentric double-pipe (copper inner 60/62 mm, L 90 cm; galvanized outer 250 mm, 75 cm). AIR forced through inner tube (blower+inverter); steam in annulus -> constant wall T 98 C. NOT liquid-liquid; effectively tube with constant wall temperature.
Insert: coiled wire e/d 0.1/0.066/0.033; p/d 0.4/0.3/0.2; l/d 15/10/5.
Data: experimental, reused from Kapan et al. 2023 (Heat Mass Transfer, Taguchi study). 162 experiments (=27 geometries x 6 Re levels presumably; Re range not reported in this paper).
Fluid: air (conventional). No nanofluid.
Inputs: Re, e/d, p/d, l/d (all dimensionless). Outputs: Nu, f, eta (TPF), separate models.
Models: MLR (robust linear), RF (Table 1 lists "Boosted Trees, leaf size 8, learners 30, learning rate 0.1" -> actually an LSBoost ensemble, labelled RF - inconsistency), SVR (Medium Gaussian, kernel scale 2.2), GPR (squared exponential/exponential isotropic, constant basis), MLP (trilayered 10-10-10 ReLU, 1000 iterations). MATLAB Regression Learner (code in appendix).
Validation: 10-fold cross-validation (random). No separate hold-out test. Hyperparameters = Regression Learner presets; no tuning described.
Scaling: Not reported (Regression Learner default standardization for SVR/GPR/NN - not stated).
Metrics (CV): Nu: MLP RMSE 3.72, R2 1.00, MAE 2.35; GPR RMSE 8.25, R2 0.98, MAE 6.01; SVR RMSE 14.97 R2 0.95; RF RMSE 14.68 R2 0.95; Linear RMSE 19.63 R2 0.91.
 f: GPR RMSE 0.00331 R2 0.94 (best RMSE!), MLP 0.00367 R2 0.95. eta: MLP RMSE 0.0458 R2 0.98; GPR 0.0614 R2 0.97.
Explainability: Pearson correlation, one- and two-way partial dependence plots.
Uncertainty: none (GPR predictive variance not used). Experimental uncertainty: not reported in this paper.
Critical: 10-fold random CV over a 27x6 factorial grid -> every held-out point has same-geometry neighbours at adjacent Re -> interpolation in Re only; no held-out geometry. MLP 10-10-10 (~280 params) on ~146 training pts per fold - R2=1.00 plausible for smooth Nu(Re) but not evidence of generalization. "Data availability: No data was used" contradictory. Mislabelled RF.
Strength: dimensionless inputs; multi-family comparison incl GPR & SVR with linear baseline; k-fold CV; partial dependence.
Relevance: DPHE geometry nominally, but air/steam constant-wall-T, inserts, no nanofluid, no operating-temperature inputs. Methodology (multi-model comparison + CV) transferable.
