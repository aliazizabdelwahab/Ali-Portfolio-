Title: ANFIS based effectiveness and number of transfer units predictions of MWCNT/water nanofluids flow in a double pipe U-bend heat exchanger
Authors: L. Syam Sundar; B. Deepanraj; Hiren K. Mewada
Year 2023; Case Studies in Thermal Engineering 43, 102645; DOI 10.1016/j.csite.2022.102645 (CC BY-NC-ND)
HX: counter-flow tube-in-tube U-bend DPHE; copper inner tube (text: do 0.019, di 0.022 - reversed), L 5 m with U-bend at 2.2 m; GI annulus.
Fluids: hot water annulus 80 C, 3 L/min fixed; cold MWCNT(COOH)/water NF 0, 0.05, 0.1, 0.3 vol% in inner tube, 3-8 L/min, inlet 25 C. Re 3751-11139.
Samples: Not reported (4 concentrations x flow levels; number of flow levels not stated).
Properties: regression fits of measured k, mu, rho, cp vs T, phi from Saleh & Sundar.
Validation of rig: water Nu within +-2.8% of Gnielinski & Notter-Sleicher.
Inputs: Re, phi (2, dimensionless/semi). Outputs: h, Nu, effectiveness, NTU (separate ANFIS per output).
Model: ANFIS (Sugeno), fuzzy C-means clustering for MFs, hybrid learning; number of rules/MFs Not reported; MATLAB implied.
Split: random 80/20 train/test. No CV. Scaling: metrics on normalized scale for h (RMSE 0.018581 normalized vs 25.6 W/m2K physical); others physical.
Metrics: h: all R(corr coeff) 0.99786, normalized RMSE 0.0186 (25.6 W/m2K = 1.98% of max); test R 0.99728 (text labels both "test" - one is training). Nu (range 20-120): all RMSE 1.775, R 0.99617; test RMSE 2.542, R 0.99338. eps (range 0.46-0.58): all RMSE 0.00175, R 0.99824; test RMSE 0.00275. NTU (range 0.45-0.49): all RMSE 0.00143, R 0.98687; test RMSE 0.00211, R 0.99524.
Also multilinear regressions Nu, eps, NTU in Re, phi (Nu SD 6.5%) and cubic polynomial "from ANFIS" with 95% bounds.
Uncertainty: experimental in supplementary (not available); no predictive uncertainty.
Critical: abstract reports "R2" but values are correlation coefficients; mislabelled train/test text; NTU varies only 0.45-0.49 so R is dominated by trivial structure; a linear model in Re, phi already explains most; eps and NTU learned separately though linked by eps-NTU relation; sample count missing; ANFIS rules not reported; with 2 inputs ANFIS has many params relative to ~24 points.
Strength: DPHE + NF + experimental + effectiveness/NTU targets; reports physical RMSE; dimensionless inputs; linear regression baseline.
