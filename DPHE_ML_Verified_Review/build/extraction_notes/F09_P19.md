Title: Machine learning regression analysis of a heat exchanger with corrugated tape turbulators, by means of second law analysis
Authors: Nevin Celik; Gongur Pusat; Beyda Tasar; Sinan Kapan; Celal Kistak
Year 2025; Thermal Science and Engineering Progress 66, 104033; DOI 10.1016/j.tsep.2025.104033
HX: concentric double-pipe (copper inner 65/67 mm, L 90 cm; outer 75 cm); AIR in inner tube (radial fan + inverter); steam in annulus, constant wall T ~100 C. Corrugated tape inserts (t/d, w/d, p/d).
Data: experimental; each configuration repeated 3 times and AVERAGED before modelling (good practice - prevents repeated-reading leakage). Number of configurations/samples: Not reported explicitly.
Inputs: Re, t/d, w/d, p/d (dimensionless). Outputs: Ns (entropy generation number), E* (exergy destruction), effectiveness eps, NTU.
Models: MLR, Lasso, Ridge, ElasticNet (linear, regularised). Lambda by cross-validation (described generically). Software: Not reported.
Validation: train/test split Not reported; Adjusted R2 reported -> metrics appear in-sample.
Metrics (Table 3): eps: Lasso R2 0.9849, RMSE 0.0109, MAPE 4.14%; ElasticNet 0.9850, MAPE 4.12%; MLR 0.9749, MAPE 5.49%. NTU: Lasso R2 0.9841 MAPE 4.97%; MLR 0.9718 MAPE 6.43%. Ns: R2 ~0.989, MAPE ~4.2%. E*: R2 ~0.989, MAPE ~2.15%.
Uncertainty: experimental Ns +-11.12%, E* +-11.0% (large) - model MAPE (4%) is below the measurement uncertainty.
Critical: no held-out evaluation reported; air/steam constant-wall-T DPHE (not liquid-liquid, no nanofluid); second-law outputs; linear models only.
Strength: averaging repeats; regularised linear baselines; dimensionless inputs; honest acknowledgement of small dataset.
