Title: Optimization of ultrasonic-excited double-pipe heat exchanger with machine learning and PSO
Authors: Morteza Esfandyari; Amin Amiri Delouei; Ali Jalai
Year 2023; International Communications in Heat and Mass Transfer 147, 106985; DOI 10.1016/j.icheatmasstransfer.2023.106985 (subscription)
HX: counter-flow concentric double-pipe, steel, L=1.2 m, inner tube D 20 mm (t=1), outer 180 mm (t=4). Hot fluid inner tube; cold water annulus fixed 164 L/h at 20 C.
Data: own experiments. Working fluids: water, Al2O3/water (alpha, 80 nm) 0.4/0.6/0.8 vol% (hot side), with/without ultrasonic (4x60 W, 40 kHz transducers).
Ranges: hot flow 113,138,180,257 L/h; hot inlet 40/50/60 C; Re(hot) ~3232-10233.
ML samples: 84 data points (consistent with 12 T x flow combos x 7 fluid/US configurations - inference, not stated). Repeated readings: Not reported.
Inputs (4, dimensional): ultrasonic on/off, hot flow, hot inlet T, volume fraction.
Outputs: Qh, Nu_h, Nu_c, NTU, effectiveness (eps-NTU efficiency).
Models: MLP (1 and 2 hidden layers; 5/8/10/15 neurons; TANSIG/LOGSIG/PURELIN; LM/SCG/BR) - best 4-10-10-? LOGSIG+LM (R2 avg 0.946); ANFIS (genfis1 gauss/tri MF 3-5; genfis2 subtractive clustering radius 0.1-0.5; hybrid best R2 0.940); then ANN-PSO and ANFIS-PSO (PSO optimizes weights/MF params).
Split: random 70/30 -> 59 train / 25 test. No validation set described; architecture chosen by "R2 (average)" -> selection likely includes test data. No CV.
Scaling: Not reported. Software: MATLAB implied (genfis, trainlm) - not explicitly stated.
Metrics (Table 3, ANN-PSO train/test/overall): R2 Qh 0.996/0.988/0.994; Nu_h 0.991/0.988/"1.976"(impossible typo); Nu_c 0.996/0.999/0.999; NTU 0.999/0.948/0.9484; eps 0.998/0.956/0.978. MSE NTU test 5.059 vs train 0.078; eps test 4.337 vs 0.178 (units/scaling unclear) -> large train-test gap = overfitting.
ANFIS-PSO test R2: Qh 0.970, Nu_h 0.997, Nu_c 0.948, NTU 0.931, eps 0.958.
Claims: max error <1% for Qh; 95% of Nu cases <1% error; max Nu error 1.94% - inconsistent with test MSE.
Abstract says "correlation coefficients >94.84%" - actually R2 values.
Validation of rig: Nu_h vs Gnielinski reasonable.
Uncertainty: no experimental uncertainty table; flowmeter +-5%. No predictive uncertainty.
Strengths: DPHE + nanofluid + experimental + operating inputs very similar to ours (hot flow, hot Tin, phi) + thermal outputs incl. NTU/effectiveness; compares two families; reports train/test separately.
Weaknesses: random split of factorial grid (each test point has neighbors in train differing by one level) = interpolation only; model selection on test; reporting errors (R2>1); effectiveness/NTU/Q learned independently though physically linked; cold side fixed (no cold-side inputs); MSE units unclear; PSO adds complexity w/o justification; 2x10 hidden ANN with 59 training points, 5 outputs.
Tier 1. Benchmark candidate (input/output similarity high).
