Title: Campaign-disjoint validation of machine-learned critical heat flux models: leakage quantification and physics-anchored residual transfer
Authors: Satya Prakash Saraswat (single author)
Year 2026; Energy and AI 26, 100889; DOI 10.1016/j.egyai.2026.100889 (OA CC BY)
Domain: critical heat flux (CHF) in uniformly heated vertical round tubes (boiling) - NOT DPHE, not single-phase.
Data: 24,579 experimental measurements from 60 experimental campaign groups (public database).
Inputs: 15 variables - 10 dimensionless groups (reduced pressure, ln density ratio, Pr_l, ln Re_l, ln We, L/D, x_in, dimensionless subcooling, ...) + 5 raw pre-crisis variables; causal audit excluded outlet variables containing measured CHF (target leakage).
Models: direct HistGradientBoosting and XGBoost (also RF, ET mentioned) vs hybrids learning multiplicative log-residual of the Bowring correlation (physics-anchored residual learning). No hyperparameter search (defaults fixed a priori).
Validation: random record split vs campaign-disjoint (group) split; fixed sealed five-campaign block; 30 held-out partitions; leakage amplification factor LAF = grouped/random MAPE; campaign-macro MAPE.
Key results: direct learner MAPE 4.69% (random) -> 11.91% (campaign-disjoint) = 2.54x; anchored hybrids grouped MAPE 3.94% and 4.96% (LAF 1.36, 1.06); anchored better in 26/30 partitions; insensitive to 6% systematic anchor error; over+20% overpredictions reduced to 0.36-0.63%.
Applicability domain: Mahalanobis distance in standardized dimensionless feature space + correlation envelope; unsupported queries flagged instead of predicted.
UQ: one-sided k95 divisors, 95/95 tolerance (descriptive; not conformal).
Relevance: different physics, but the single most relevant evidence for (i) grouped/held-out-condition validation, (ii) correlation-anchored residual learning, (iii) applicability-domain gating. Directly transferable to our DPHE design (group = operating condition / concentration).
Tier 3 (supporting methodology) - high methodological value.
