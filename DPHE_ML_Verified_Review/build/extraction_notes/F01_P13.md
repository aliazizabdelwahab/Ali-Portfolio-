Title: Prediction of overall heat transfer coefficient in concentric tube heat exchangers using artificial neural networks: A comparative study with empirical correlations
Authors: Ahmed Mohsin Alsayah; Mohammed J. Alshukri; Samer Ali; Jalal Faraj; Mahmoud Khaled
Year 2025; International Communications in Heat and Mass Transfer 163, 108723; DOI 10.1016/j.icheatmasstransfer.2025.108723
HX: counter-flow concentric tube (2D axisymmetric CFD, ANSYS Fluent, k-omega SST; mesh GCI 0.375%); validated vs Taler/Gnielinski etc. (0.4-17% differences).
Data: 2,700 CFD simulations: Re 1,000-20,000 each side, fluid pairs air/air, air/water, water/air, water/water (365/280 K or 600/280 K inlets), Di 0.01-0.05 m, Do/Di 1.25/1.5/3, L 0.4-4 m. Transitional Re 2300-4000 removed (final N after filtering: Not reported precisely). No nanofluids.
Inputs (11): Re_h, Re_c, Pr_h, Pr_c, Di, Do/Di, L, k_h, k_c, mu_h, mu_c (mixed dimensionless + dimensional property inputs). Output: U (overall HTC).
Preprocessing: StandardScaler (fit scope before/after split: Not reported); filtering of transitional data.
Split: 80% train+val / 20% held-out test (random); 5-fold CV on the 80% for architecture selection; then retrain on 80% and evaluate test once. (Proper separation of model selection and test.)
Models: 6 MLP architectures (16-32-16 ... 128-256-128; 4-layer variants); best Model 4: 128-256-128 ReLU, ReLU output, L2 0.001, Adam lr 0.001, MAE loss, early stopping patience 200, max 2000 epochs; Keras/TensorFlow.
Metrics: CV val MAPE best 4.96% (+-0.67); test MAE 5.503 W/m2K, test MAPE 3.08%; APE median 0.33% (train+test pooled); 75th pct <1.5%. Regime-wise median APE NN 0.1-0.7% vs correlations 4.8-25.8%.
Baselines: Baehr-Stephan, Taler, Dittus-Boelter, Petukhov-Kirillov, Gnielinski, Colburn.
Explainability: SHAP PermutationExplainer (k_c, Re_h, mu_h, Di most important).
Uncertainty: none for predictions; CFD discretization error reported.
Critical: random split of a dense CFD grid = interpolation; correlations comparison partly unfair (NN trained on same CFD; correlations not fitted); SHAP highlights k_c which is effectively a fluid-pair indicator; deep net (~66k params) justified only because 2,700 noise-free samples. Not experimental, no nanofluid.
Strength: best-reported ML pipeline among DPHE papers (CV for selection, untouched test, physical-unit MAE/MAPE, correlation baselines, SHAP, regularization).
Relevance: methodology highly transferable (pipeline/reporting), data source not.
Tier 2. Supporting benchmark for pipeline reporting.
