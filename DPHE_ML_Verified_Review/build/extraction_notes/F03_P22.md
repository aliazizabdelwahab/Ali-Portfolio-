Title: Enhancing thermal efficiency in twisted tri-lobe double pipe heat exchangers via integrated CFD and AI approaches
Authors: Aliakbar Sheikhi Azizi; Milad Razbin; S. Morteza Mousavi; Ming Li; A. Ali Rabienataj Darzi
Year 2024; International Journal of Thermal Sciences 206, 109331; DOI 10.1016/j.ijthermalsci.2024.109331
HX: DPHE with twisted tri-lobe inner and outer tubes; water; CFD (Fluent, k-omega SST; validated vs Tang et al. experiments).
Data: CFD; RSM reduced 768 permutations to 41 cases (Table S1). Inputs (5): Re_outer, Re_inner (10,000-25,000), outer & inner twist ratio (5-20), twist direction. Outputs: Y1 Nu_o, Y2 Nu_i, Y3 f_o, Y4 f_i -> Y5 PEC.
Preprocessing: min-max to [0.1, 0.9]. Split: 36 train / 5 test; no validation set ("omitted due to limited data"); TGF (train+test R2 & MSE) used to tune hyperparameters -> test used for selection.
Model: 4 separate single-hidden-layer FFBP ANNs (tansig), LM; MATLAB; also RSM quadratic models (lower performance).
Metrics: R2 ~1 train and test (figures); weights published in supplementary tables.
Extrapolation: GA optimisation beyond training domain (Re 5,000-50,000; twist 2.5-25) followed by CFD simulations of AI-guided cases -> trends confirmed but authors acknowledge "variance between predicted and simulated cost functions ... attributed to extrapolation" (quantitative error not tabulated clearly). One of the few papers that actually TESTS extrapolated predictions against new simulations.
Uncertainty: none.
Critical: CFD deterministic; 5 test points; test used in model selection; extrapolation ok for trend but accuracy degraded.
Tier 3 (CFD; useful evidence on extrapolation degradation and confirmation simulations).
